Quick answers

Can you give me personalised medical advice?

No. Our team cannot diagnose or treat individual conditions by email. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional who can assess you properly.

I spotted a factual error. What should I do?

Choose “Report an error” in the form and include the article title and the specific detail. We investigate every correction request and update the article if needed.

Do you accept guest posts or advertising?

We do not publish sponsored health claims or paid placements. For editorial collaborations or press enquiries, choose “Partnership or press”.
