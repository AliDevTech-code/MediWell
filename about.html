About us

Health information you can trust

MediWell exists to make reliable health knowledge understandable, practical and free for everyone.

Our mission

Clear answers in a world of health noise

The internet is full of conflicting health advice. Our goal is simple: publish accurate, evidence-based information in language that anyone can understand — without hype, fear or hidden agendas.

Every article is written by our editorial team, checked against guidance from major public health bodies and peer-reviewed research, and reviewed for clinical accuracy. When the evidence is uncertain, we say so.

What we stand for

Our editorial values

Three principles guide everything we publish.

Evidence first

We prioritise systematic reviews, clinical guidelines and large studies. We link claims to the strength of the evidence, not to headlines.

Plain language

No jargon for jargon's sake. If a medical term is necessary, we explain it. Clarity is a form of respect for the reader.

Reader independence

We do not sell health products or accept payment for favourable coverage. Our only obligation is to the people reading.

Editorial policy

How our content is made

A simple, transparent process from research to publication.

Step 1

Research & writing

Writers review current guidance from WHO, CDC, NHS and peer-reviewed sources, then draft in plain English with a clear takeaway.

Step 2

Clinical review

A qualified clinician checks factual accuracy, safety wording and whether the advice is appropriate for a general audience.

Step 3

Publish & update

Each article shows a review date. We revisit content when guidelines change or new evidence becomes available.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I use MediWell instead of visiting a doctor?

No. Our content is educational only. It cannot diagnose or treat a condition, and it does not replace a personalised assessment by a qualified healthcare professional.

How do you keep articles accurate?

We use current public health guidance and peer-reviewed research, then subject every article to a clinical review before publishing. Articles are periodically revisited and updated.

Do the calculators store my data?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are never transmitted to a server or shared with third parties.

Is MediWell free?

Yes, completely. We do not use paywalls and we do not sell health products or reader data.

How can I suggest a topic or report an error?

Use the contact form and choose the relevant subject. We read every message.
